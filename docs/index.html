DillyDally is a free menu-bar app that casts your Mac's screen to a TV over DLNA, in up to 4K at 60 frames a second, with sound. It's for when the TV doesn't support AirPlay and you don't have a cable at hand.

macOS sees your TV as a display

The display is called DillyDally, and the Screen Mirroring menu you already know decides what goes on it. The TV can mirror your whole screen, show one window, or be a second desktop.

The movie plays on through Wi-Fi hiccups

The TV plays about two seconds behind your Mac. That's a buffer of video it hasn't shown yet. When the Wi-Fi has a hiccup, the TV plays from the buffer instead of freezing. Microwaves and Wi-Fi share a frequency, which makes popcorn a good test.

Buffer

If your Wi-Fi is worse than a microwave, raise the buffer to five or ten seconds.

For when you can't find the HDMI cable

You want to watch a movie on that weird older TV, the one with no AirPlay. The HDMI cable isn't where you left it. DillyDally gets the movie there over Wi-Fi, so you can stop looking. If the cable shows up, it's about two seconds faster, and you can plug one in here to compare.

Made for movies, terrible at Pong

A movie has no idea it's two seconds late. A TV show, the playoffs, your vacation photos and Monday's slides don't either. A game finds out right away, and a round of Pong on the TV will show you how.

Try it with your own screen

This page can play your real screen on its TV. Your browser asks which screen, window or tab to share, and nothing you share leaves the browser.

How it works

Your TV already plays video files from the network. DillyDally makes your screen look like one that never ends, so the TV needs nothing installed and the video stays on your own network.

Install on your Mac

DillyDally is free and open source. Choose how to install it.

Download DillyDally.dmg

Open the disk image and drag DillyDally to Applications. Apple hasn't notarized the app, so run the line below once before you open it.

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/DillyDally.app
zsh

    
  

  
The app also comes with dillydally, a command-line tool that casts without the menu bar. The terminal above understands it, so type a command.
